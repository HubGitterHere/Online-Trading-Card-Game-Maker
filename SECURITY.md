## Reporting a Vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities by contacting the team in our chat on google teams: 
https://teams.microsoft.com/l/chat/19%3A06f204c3c77043b69ccd847dc7c1dd21%40thread.v2/conversations?context=%7B%22contextType%22%3A%22chat%22%7D
Replies are infrequent but will be given as soon as possible.
Expect confirmation message upon vulnerabilities being resolved or declined.
